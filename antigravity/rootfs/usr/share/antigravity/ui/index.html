Google Antigravity OAuth Setup Guide

To connect your Google account, you can supply your OAuth refresh_token or full oauth_creds.json in the Home Assistant Addon Configuration.

1

Locate Existing Google / Antigravity Credentials

If you use the Antigravity IDE, Gemini CLI, or Google Cloud SDK, your credentials are saved locally at:

Windows: C:\Users\<Username>\.gemini\antigravity\ Or: %APPDATA%\gcloud\application_default_credentials.json Linux/Mac: ~/.config/gcloud/application_default_credentials.json
2

Generate a New Refresh Token via gcloud

You can run this terminal command on your workstation to authenticate:

gcloud auth application-default login

Copy the refresh_token string from the generated JSON file.

4

Save in Home Assistant Addon Settings

Go to Home Assistant → Settings → Add-ons → Antigravity → Configuration, paste your token under accounts, and click Save!
