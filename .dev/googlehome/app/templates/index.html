Step 2: Obtain E2EE Decryption Key (Shared Key)
Google encrypts GPS tracker coordinates end-to-end. Click below for instructions on retrieving your 64-character hex shared key via Google Domain Unlock or DevTools.
💡 Tip: The Google Unlock page must be opened in a browser where your Google Account is already signed in. If it loops or hangs on desktop, approve the screen lock on your Android phone or use DevTools Network tab to copy the key.
Step 3: Deploy to Home Assistant
